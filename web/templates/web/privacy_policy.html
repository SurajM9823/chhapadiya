Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

At Chhapadiya Traders, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website www.chhapadiyatraders.com or make purchases through our e-commerce platform.

By accessing or using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Personal Information you provide to us:

  • Name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address
  • Account login credentials (if you create an account)
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment gateways)
  • Order details and purchase history
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide (reviews, support queries, etc.)

Automatically Collected Information:

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and operating system
  • Browsing behavior, pages visited, time spent on the site
  • Cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies

Information from Third Parties:

Payment processors, delivery partners, and analytics services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To communicate with you about orders, updates, and customer service
  • To improve our website, products, and services
  • To personalize your shopping experience
  • To send promotional offers, newsletters, and marketing communications (you can opt-out anytime)
  • For security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance
  • To conduct analytics and understand user behavior

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: Payment gateways (e.g., eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, Stripe), logistics/delivery partners, hosting providers, and email service providers.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government authority in Nepal.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. We may also use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand website traffic.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable Nepali law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Since our operations are primarily based in Nepal, your data is mainly processed within Nepal. However, some third-party service providers may be located outside Nepal.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a new effective date.
